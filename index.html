03 · Simple design, advanced technology

Triaxial engagement

Rotate the top plate into place and it locks against vertical, horizontal and lateral load at once — a mechanically engaged connection, then the pins go in. No slop, no creep, nothing to feel but the snow.

Universal fit

Top plates are pre-drilled for the most popular alpine, touring and telemark patterns. Your binding of choice, any day of the week.

Dynamic mount point

Mount up to ±2 cm* off the recommended line. Park laps at +1.5, big lines at 0 — swapped in minutes, on the same ski.
